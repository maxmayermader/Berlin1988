## Phase Goal

**As a** group of players, **I want to** go from the home page through a lobby into a complete 14-round match and see a result, **so that** we can actually play Berlin 1988 in a browser.

This plan delivers the second vertical slice: *ready up and start the match, with AI filling the empty chairs*.

<objective>
Turn the join-only room from Plan 01-01 into a working lobby: every seat's ready state visible to everyone, an automatic countdown at the >=50% threshold, AI opponents filling empty seats at start, and the single `LOADOUT` to `IN_GAME` transition that calls `createMatch()` with this phase's locked settings.

Purpose: this is the seam where lobby bookkeeping becomes a real `GameState`. RESEARCH.md Pitfall 5 identifies lobby readiness as a read-then-write race class, and D-07's bot-fill timing (at match start, never eagerly) is the specific decision that avoids it. Getting the transition wrong here means every later plan is working against a match that was never correctly configured.

Output: a lobby a solo host can start against three bots, or four humans can start together, landing in `RoomState.phase === 'IN_GAME'` with a real `GameState` and each seat holding the PHANTOM loadout.
</objective>
