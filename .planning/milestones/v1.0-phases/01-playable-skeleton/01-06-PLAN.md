## Phase Goal

**As a** group of players, **I want to** go from the home page through a lobby into a complete 14-round match and see a result, **so that** we can actually play Berlin 1988 in a browser.

This plan delivers the final vertical slice: *the match ends, and the game tells you who won and why* — and then proves the whole phase against a real deployed room.

<objective>
Close Phase 1: render the result screen from the engine's own `MatchOutcome`, run the four-player concurrent-timing measurement that `ROADMAP.md` flagged as the phase's critical unknown, deploy the room, and confirm against a live Durable Object that it survives hibernation across the 90-second order window.

Purpose: the last two ROADMAP.md research flags land here. Concurrent timing at n=4 has never been measured — the engine is validated at p99 under 50ms per bot decision but nothing in the repo has ever had four connections queuing messages at one room. And hibernation, per `01-RESEARCH.md` Axis 3, structurally cannot be exercised in local dev, which means Plan 01-01's `onMessage`/`onClose` rule and Plan 01-03's storage rehydration are both untested claims until a deployed room has been idled and then woken.

Output: a match that ends properly, a deployed stack, and two measurements written down rather than assumed.
</objective>
