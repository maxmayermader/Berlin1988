<objective>
Expand the tracer's history rows from bare round numbers into the deduction surface MATCH-06 actually
promises: each row says what happened, and clicking it re-runs that round's resolution through the
same `StepThrough` renderer the live game uses.

Purpose: this is the half of MATCH-06 a player uses to *reason*. A list of fourteen numbered rows is
not a deduction surface; "Round 6 — 2 agents burned" is. Reusing `StepThrough` rather than writing a
second renderer is D-03's explicit instruction and keeps the fixed published priority order (MATCH-05's
guarantee from Phase 1) true of history as well as of live play.

Output: `roundHeadline`/`roundNumberOf` in `apps/web/lib/format.ts`, an optional `mode` prop on
`StepThrough`, and the finished `RoundHistoryPanel`.
</objective>
