<objective>
MATCH-07: the player's own Burn Track, shown exactly as opponents see it, as the second tab of the
Intel drawer the tracer already built.

This is the phase's one slice that needs **zero** engine or protocol work. `PlayerView.burnTracks`
already carries every player's track including the viewer's own, identically suppressed, populated
incrementally during the round pipeline by `appendBurn()` — verified end to end in RESEARCH.md across
`createMatch.ts`, `ctx.ts`, `projectView.ts` and `view.ts`. The only thing missing is the surface.

Purpose: the Burn Track is a *capability profile*, not an exhaustion counter (`docs/GAME_DESIGN.md`
§6.3). Its whole design value is that a player can audit their own tells — see the pattern their card
plays have painted, notice that a Cutout blanked one of them, and adjust. Rendering it wrong (a raw
object dump, or a synthesized tag announcing what was hidden) destroys exactly the deduction the
mechanic exists to create.

Output: `apps/web/lib/burnTrack.ts`, `apps/web/components/intel/BurnTrackPanel.tsx`, and the Intel
drawer's two-tab switcher with its new-entry badge.
</objective>
