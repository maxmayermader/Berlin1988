## Phase Goal

**As a** player, **I want to** build and keep my own 10-card loadout and take it into a match, **so that** I play my own deck instead of the default the skeleton shipped with.

This plan delivers the last vertical slice and closes the phase: *change my mind at the table, and play the deck I changed it to*.

<objective>
Put the same deckbuilder inside the lobby. A seated player opens it, drops out of ready so no countdown can start underneath them, rebuilds, saves, readies again, and plays the deck they just built — without leaving the room, dropping their socket, or losing their seat.

Purpose: DECK-05 is the requirement that makes the deckbuilder part of the game rather than a page beside it. The last thing anyone does before a match is look at who they are playing and reconsider their deck, and D-05 is what stops that reconsideration from racing the countdown. The interesting work is not the rendering — the component is finished — it is the two states that only exist here: a save the room can refuse, and a local deck that has drifted from the one the room holds.

Output: the in-lobby embed, the D-05 ready clear, a save control gated on legality and reconciled against the room's reply, an honest notice when the two copies of the deck disagree, and a two-context Playwright spec that walks the whole flow.
</objective>

<planner_assumptions>
Flagged for the record — surfaced, not dropped.

- **The `Back to Lobby` exit and its divergence notice are planner additions, not artifact requirements.** 02-UI-SPEC.md specifies only the `Save Loadout` CTA, disabled until the deck is legal. Taken alone that traps a player who breaks their deck inside the editor with no way out, in a room where a countdown may be running. A second, always-enabled exit resolves it, and it introduces exactly one new state — a local deck that differs from the room's — which this plan makes visible rather than silent. The notice copy is planner's choice; the contract has no row for a state its design did not contain.
- **The unconditional ready clear and the always-shown banner are one decision.** 02-UI-SPEC.md requires the banner on every open and "never conditionally hidden". Clearing ready only when the seat happened to be ready would make the banner tell some players something untrue. Clearing unconditionally costs one idempotent write and keeps the contract honest.
- **RESEARCH Open Question 2 is resolved toward in-place conditional rendering, per the researcher's own recommendation.** A `/lobby/[code]/deck` route would read more cleanly but tears down `useRoomSocket`'s connection on navigation and forces a token rebind mid-lobby for no user-visible benefit.
- **The e2e cannot assert which deck the match dealt, and does not pretend to.** Playwright has no access to `GameState`, so `lobby.spec.ts` asserts the flow reaches the match and `apps/party/tests/loadout.test.ts` asserts the deck — the same two-harness split, with the same seam closed the same way, as Plan 02-01's tracer.
- **Neither exit re-readies the player.** D-05 requires re-confirmation and 02-UI-SPEC.md states the CTA "does not re-ready the player". A convenience auto-ready on save would defeat the point of clearing it.
